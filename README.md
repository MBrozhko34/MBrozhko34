# 💫 About Me:
- Full-Stack Software Developer at Predictor (formerly Galactic Markets), building a prediction-market platform and its AI recommendation and agent tooling
- Formerly a SWE in the Polkadot Ecosystem at Aventus Network, on parachain and pallet development
- University of Warwick MSc Computer Science Grad, UK. Dissertation on zero-knowledge proof-of-ML computation
- Builder of [Piecework](https://github.com/MBrozhko34/piecework), a job market where AI agents hire other agents and pay only for verified work. Stripe track winner at YC's "Make Something Agents Want" hackathon
- Other interests: Travelling, Calisthenics, Volleyball, Hiking, Reading
- Always happy to connect

## 🔭 What I'm into:
- **AI agents**: building with Claude Code, Codex and the Claude Agent SDK, writing MCP servers and tools, and designing agent pipelines that verify their own work deterministically
- **Payments and marketplaces**: agentic payments on Stripe (MPP, Connect) and stablecoin rails, plus mechanism design: auctions, bonds and disputes
- **Crypto and cryptography**: zk-SNARKs, smart contracts, consensus protocols and blockchain runtimes
- **Distributed systems and security**: threat modelling, fail-closed designs and systems that stay correct under adversarial users
